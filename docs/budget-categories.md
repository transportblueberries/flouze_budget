# Recommended budget categories for students

This document proposes the default set of spending categories Flouze should
offer. The goal is a list that is short enough to stay usable, generic enough
to work at any university, and concrete enough that a student can classify an
expense in a few seconds.

## Design principles

- **Few categories, clear names.** Every category below answers one plain
  question ("what was this for?"). If two categories would usually be confused,
  they are merged.
- **Needs before wants.** Categories are grouped into essentials, study,
  everyday life, and future, so the app can show how much of a budget goes to
  fixed obligations versus flexible spending.
- **Income-independent.** The list works whether money comes from a job,
  family support, a scholarship, or a loan, and in any currency.

## The categories

### Essentials

| Category | Covers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Housing | Rent and the costs of keeping a place to live | Monthly rent, dorm fees, utilities, household supplies |
| Groceries | Food and basics bought to prepare at home | Supermarket shopping, market produce, toiletries bought with groceries |
| Transport | Getting to campus, work, and around the city | Transit pass, bus/train tickets, bike repairs, occasional fuel or ride shares |
| Insurance / health | Staying covered and cared for | Health insurance premium, pharmacy, doctor or dentist copays, glasses |
| Phone / internet | Staying reachable and connected | Mobile plan, home internet share, prepaid top-ups |

### Study

| Category | Covers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | Direct costs of studying | Tuition instalments, exam and course fees, textbooks, printing, software needed for coursework |

### Everyday life

| Category | Covers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eating out | Food bought ready to eat (kept separate from groceries because it is usually the flexible part of food spending) | Cafeteria meals, coffee, restaurants, food delivery |
| Leisure | Fun and social life | Cinema, concerts, sports, hobbies, streaming-free one-off events, travel for pleasure |
| Subscriptions | Recurring digital or membership charges | Music/video streaming, cloud storage, gym membership, app subscriptions |
| Personal | Clothing, care, and small one-offs that fit nowhere else | Haircut, clothes, gifts, household gadgets |

### Future and the unexpected

| Category | Covers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Savings | Money deliberately set aside, treated as a planned "expense" so it actually happens | Emergency fund, a planned trip or purchase, a term-deposit transfer |
| Other / unexpected | Anything genuinely irregular that should not distort the categories above | Replacing a broken phone, a lost-item fee, an unplanned trip home |

## Notes for future product work

- Eating out is split from groceries on purpose: for most students it is the
  single largest controllable overspend, and merging it into groceries hides it.
- Subscriptions are their own category, not part of leisure, so the app can
  later surface a recurring-charges overview.
- "Other / unexpected" is a safety valve, not a junk drawer: if one kind of
  expense keeps landing there, it deserves its own category in a future
  revision of this list.
- Users should be able to rename or hide categories, but these defaults should
  stay stable so statistics remain comparable over time.
