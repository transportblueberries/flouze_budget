Flouze Budget

Simple money tools for students

Plan a monthly budget, explore the 50/30/20 reference, estimate a savings goal, or track a shared trip or project.

Private by default. Your financial data stays in your browser. Flouze does not send it to a server.

Educational tools only — not personalised financial advice.

Trip / project budget

Track a shared trip, move or event. Convert every expense to the display currency, then calculate each participant's fair share and reimbursements.

Participants and split weights

Use equal weights such as 1 / 1 for a 50/50 split, or values such as 45.7 / 54.3 for an income-weighted split.

The exchange rate is manual in this version. Example: if 1 EUR = 0.95 CHF, enter 0.95 when CHF is the display currency.
