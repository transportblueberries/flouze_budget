# Code of Conduct

Flouze Budget is intended to be a welcoming, respectful and constructive open-source project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- being respectful and considerate;
- welcoming people with different levels of technical experience;
- giving constructive feedback;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- respecting privacy and personal boundaries;
- accepting reasonable correction when mistakes are made.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation or discrimination;
- insulting, demeaning or hostile comments;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption, spam or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions or interactions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

If you need to report conduct privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue.
